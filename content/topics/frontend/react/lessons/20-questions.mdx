# Practice & Interview Questions

> Attempt these on your own — no answers are provided. Build, run, and verify.

## Practice tasks

**Easy**
1. Build a counter with `useState`.
2. Render a list from an array with proper `key`s.
3. Build a controlled form input.

**Medium**
4. Fetch and display data with `useEffect` (handle loading/error).
5. Build a todo app with add/toggle/delete.
6. Create a custom hook (e.g. `useLocalStorage`).
7. Share state with `useContext`.

## Interview questions

1. What are props vs state?
2. What is the virtual DOM and reconciliation?
3. Why do lists need a `key`, and why not the index?
4. Explain the rules of hooks.
5. What does the `useEffect` dependency array control?
6. What causes unnecessary re-renders and how do you prevent them (`memo`, `useMemo`, `useCallback`)?
7. Controlled vs uncontrolled components?
