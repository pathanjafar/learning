# Practice & Interview Questions

> Attempt these on your own — no answers are provided. Work them out, then verify or discuss.

## Coding practice

**Easy**
1. Generate all subsets of a set.
2. Generate all permutations of an array.
3. Letter combinations of a phone number.

**Medium**
4. Combination sum.
5. Generate valid parentheses.
6. Word search on a grid.
7. Palindrome partitioning.
8. Subsets with duplicates (no repeated subsets).

**Hard**
9. N-Queens.
10. Sudoku solver.
11. Word search II.
12. Restore IP addresses.

## Interview questions

1. Describe the choose → explore → un-choose template.
2. What role does pruning play in backtracking's practicality?
3. How is backtracking different from plain brute force / DFS?
4. How do you avoid generating duplicate results?
5. What's the typical time complexity, and why is it often exponential?
