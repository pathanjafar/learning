# Practice & Interview Questions

> Attempt these on your own — no answers are provided. Work them out, then verify or discuss.

## Coding practice

**Easy**
1. Reverse a singly linked list.
2. Detect a cycle in a linked list.
3. Merge two sorted linked lists.
4. Find the middle node.
5. Remove duplicates from a sorted list.

**Medium**
6. Remove the Nth node from the end (one pass).
7. Reorder list (L0→Ln→L1→Ln-1…).
8. Add two numbers represented as linked lists.
9. Linked list cycle II (return the cycle's start).
10. Odd–even linked list.

**Hard**
11. Reverse nodes in K-groups.
12. Merge K sorted lists.
13. Copy a list with random pointers.

## Interview questions

1. Why use a dummy head node?
2. How do fast/slow pointers find the middle and detect cycles?
3. Compare arrays vs linked lists for access, insertion, and memory.
4. How do you reverse a list in place in O(1) space?
5. When is a doubly linked list worth the extra pointer?
