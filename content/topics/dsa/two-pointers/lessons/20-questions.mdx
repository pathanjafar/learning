# Practice & Interview Questions

> Attempt these on your own — no answers are provided. Work them out, then verify or discuss.

## Coding practice

**Easy**
1. Two Sum II (input array is sorted).
2. Valid palindrome using two pointers.
3. Reverse an array in place.
4. Merge two sorted arrays in place.
5. Squares of a sorted array.

**Medium**
6. 3Sum (find all unique triplets summing to zero).
7. Container with most water.
8. Remove duplicates from a sorted array in place.
9. Sort colors (Dutch national flag).
10. Partition an array around a pivot.

**Hard**
11. Trapping rain water.
12. 4Sum.

## Interview questions

1. When can you use the converging two-pointer technique, and what's the prerequisite?
2. How do fast/slow pointers detect a cycle?
3. Why does two pointers reduce O(n²) to O(n) for sorted-array pair problems?
4. Contrast the two-pointer and sliding-window patterns.
5. How do you avoid duplicate triplets in 3Sum?
