# Practice & Interview Questions

> Attempt these on your own — no answers are provided. Work them out, then verify or discuss.

## Coding practice

**Easy**
1. Implement a trie (insert / search / startsWith).
2. Count words that start with a given prefix.

**Medium**
3. Design an autocomplete / search-suggestions system.
4. Replace words with their shortest root (dictionary).
5. Add and search word with `.` wildcard.
6. Longest word in a dictionary buildable one char at a time.

**Hard**
7. Word search II (trie + DFS on a board).
8. Maximum XOR of two numbers in an array (bitwise trie).
9. Palindrome pairs.

## Interview questions

1. What's the time/space cost of trie insert and search?
2. When does a trie beat a hash set?
3. How do you mark the end of a word in a trie node?
4. How would you implement prefix autocomplete with a trie?
5. What is a bitwise trie and what problems use it?
