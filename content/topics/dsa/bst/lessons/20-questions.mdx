# Practice & Interview Questions

> Attempt these on your own — no answers are provided. Work them out, then verify or discuss.

## Coding practice

**Easy**
1. Search a value in a BST.
2. Insert a value into a BST.
3. Find the minimum / maximum value.
4. Range sum of a BST.

**Medium**
5. Validate a binary search tree.
6. Kth smallest element in a BST.
7. Lowest common ancestor in a BST (use the ordering).
8. Convert a sorted array to a balanced BST.
9. Delete a node from a BST.
10. Inorder successor of a node.

**Hard**
11. Recover a BST with two swapped nodes.
12. Count of smaller numbers after self (BST / BIT).

## Interview questions

1. What invariant defines a BST?
2. Why does an inorder traversal of a BST yield sorted values?
3. What degrades a BST to O(n), and how do self-balancing trees fix it?
4. Outline the three cases of BST deletion.
5. When would you use a TreeMap/TreeSet over a HashMap/HashSet?
