# Practice & Interview Questions

> Attempt these on your own — no answers are provided. Work them out, then verify or discuss.

## Coding practice

**Easy**
1. Maximum sum subarray of fixed size K.
2. Average of all subarrays of size K.
3. Find the smallest subarray with a sum ≥ target.

**Medium**
4. Longest substring without repeating characters.
5. Longest substring with at most K distinct characters.
6. Permutation in string (does s2 contain a permutation of s1?).
7. Fruit into baskets.
8. Max consecutive ones III (flip at most K zeros).
9. Subarrays with K different integers.

**Hard**
10. Minimum window substring.
11. Sliding window maximum (using a deque).
12. Longest substring with at most two distinct characters.

## Interview questions

1. How do you decide between a fixed-size and a variable-size window?
2. Why is the sliding window O(n) even with the inner shrink loop?
3. What data structure tracks window contents for "distinct count" problems?
4. How do you recognize a sliding-window problem from its wording?
5. When does a deque help inside a window?
