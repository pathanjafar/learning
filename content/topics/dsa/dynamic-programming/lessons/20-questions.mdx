# Practice & Interview Questions

> Attempt these on your own — no answers are provided. Work them out, then verify or discuss.

## Coding practice

**Easy**
1. Climbing stairs.
2. House robber.
3. Min cost climbing stairs.
4. Fibonacci with memoization.

**Medium**
5. Coin change (fewest coins for an amount).
6. Longest increasing subsequence.
7. Unique paths in a grid.
8. Word break.
9. Longest common subsequence.
10. Partition equal subset sum.

**Hard**
11. Edit distance.
12. 0/1 knapsack.
13. Regular expression matching.
14. Burst balloons.

## Interview questions

1. What two properties signal that a problem needs DP?
2. Contrast top-down memoization with bottom-up tabulation.
3. How do you define the "state" for a DP, and why is it the hard part?
4. How can you reduce a 2-D DP to O(n) space?
5. How do you reconstruct the actual solution (not just its value) from a DP table?
