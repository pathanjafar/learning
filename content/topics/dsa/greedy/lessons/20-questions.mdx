# Practice & Interview Questions

> Attempt these on your own — no answers are provided. Work them out, then verify or discuss.

## Coding practice

**Easy**
1. Assign cookies to children.
2. Best time to buy and sell stock II (multiple transactions).
3. Lemonade change.

**Medium**
4. Jump game (can you reach the end?).
5. Gas station.
6. Task scheduler.
7. Partition labels.
8. Non-overlapping intervals (remove fewest).
9. Merge intervals.

**Hard**
10. Candy distribution.
11. Jump game II (minimum jumps).
12. Minimum number of taps to water a garden.

## Interview questions

1. What is the greedy-choice property, and how do you know greedy is correct?
2. Give a problem where greedy fails and DP is needed — why?
3. How does sorting often set up a greedy solution?
4. Describe an exchange argument for proving greedy optimality.
5. Compare greedy vs dynamic programming in approach and cost.
