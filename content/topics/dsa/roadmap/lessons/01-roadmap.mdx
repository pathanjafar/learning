# 15-Week DSA Roadmap

A guided path from fundamentals to interview-ready. Each week links to the topic to study; read the
lessons, then grind the curated LeetCode set in that topic. Adjust the pace to your schedule — the
**order** matters more than the exact weeks.

## Phase 1 — Fundamentals (Weeks 1–2)
- Complexity & Big-O, then arrays and strings.
- 👉 [Arrays](/tracks/dsa/arrays) (start with the Big-O lesson) · [Strings](/tracks/dsa/strings)
- Pair patterns: [Two Pointers](/tracks/dsa/two-pointers) · [Sliding Window](/tracks/dsa/sliding-window)

## Phase 2 — Linear Data Structures (Weeks 3–4)
- 👉 [Hashing](/tracks/dsa/hashing) · [Stacks & Queues](/tracks/dsa/stack-queue) · [Linked List](/tracks/dsa/linked-list)

## Phase 3 — Non-linear Data Structures (Weeks 5–6)
- 👉 [Recursion](/tracks/dsa/recursion) · [Trees](/tracks/dsa/trees) · [Binary Search Trees](/tracks/dsa/bst)
- Begin [Graphs](/tracks/dsa/graphs) (BFS/DFS basics).

## Phase 4 — Sorting, Searching & Greedy (Weeks 7–8)
- Binary search variants and sorting algorithms (covered in [Arrays](/tracks/dsa/arrays) and [Advanced](/tracks/dsa/advanced)).
- 👉 [Greedy](/tracks/dsa/greedy)

## Phase 5 — Dynamic Programming & Backtracking (Weeks 9–10)
- 👉 [Dynamic Programming](/tracks/dsa/dynamic-programming) — the big one; do it slowly.
- 👉 [Backtracking](/tracks/dsa/backtracking)

## Phase 6 — Advanced Graphs (Weeks 11–12)
- 👉 [Graphs](/tracks/dsa/graphs) — topological sort, Dijkstra/Bellman-Ford, MST, Union-Find.

## Phase 7 — Specialized Structures (Weeks 13–14)
- 👉 [Heaps / Priority Queue](/tracks/dsa/heaps) · [Tries](/tracks/dsa/tries)
- 👉 [Advanced](/tracks/dsa/advanced) — Union-Find, bit manipulation, segment tree / BIT.

## Phase 8 — Practice & Interview Prep (Week 15+)
- Mixed problem sets, timed mock interviews, and revisiting weak patterns.
- Use each topic's **Practice & Interview Questions** and **Curated LeetCode Practice** lessons.

## How to study each topic

1. Read **Concept** → **Techniques/Patterns** lessons.
2. Skim the **Cheatsheet** (where present) for quick recall.
3. Solve the **Curated LeetCode** set, easy → hard. Don't peek at solutions for ~20 minutes.
4. Review the **Pattern recognition** notes so you can spot the technique under pressure.

> Consistency beats intensity: 1–2 problems a day for 15 weeks turns into ~200 solved problems and
> real pattern fluency.
