# Practice & Interview Questions

> Attempt these on your own — no answers are provided. Work them out, then verify or discuss.

## Coding practice

**Easy**
1. Factorial and Fibonacci recursively.
2. Sum of digits of a number.
3. Reverse a string recursively.
4. Power function `pow(x, n)`.

**Medium**
5. Generate all subsets of a set.
6. Generate all permutations of an array.
7. Tower of Hanoi.
8. Flatten a nested list.
9. Merge sort / quick sort.

**Hard**
10. Regular expression matching.
11. N-th Catalan structure (e.g. valid parentheses count).
12. Recursive expression evaluation.

## Interview questions

1. What are the two required parts of any recursion?
2. How does the call stack work, and what causes a stack overflow?
3. When is recursion clearer than iteration, and when is it worse?
4. What is tail recursion and why doesn't Python optimize it?
5. How does memoization turn exponential recursion into polynomial?
