# Practice & Interview Questions

> Attempt these on your own — no answers are provided. Configure, reload, and verify.

## Practice tasks

**Easy**
1. Serve a static site from a directory.
2. Configure a reverse proxy to a backend on `localhost:3000`.
3. Redirect HTTP to HTTPS.

**Medium**
4. Terminate TLS with a certificate and proxy to an app.
5. Route `/api/` to one service and `/` to another.
6. Enable gzip compression and basic caching headers.
7. Add a simple rate limit to an endpoint.

## Interview questions

1. What is a reverse proxy and why use one?
2. How does Nginx handle many concurrent connections efficiently?
3. What does TLS termination mean?
4. `location` block matching — how does precedence work?
5. How do you load-balance across multiple backends?
6. Forward proxy vs reverse proxy?
7. How do you reload config without dropping connections?
